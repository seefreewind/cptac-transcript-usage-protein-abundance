# Novelty and competition

The literature audit is a targeted reading of primary papers and one 2026 meeting abstract. It is not a claim that every related paper was found.

Pan-cancer isoform switching and predicted domain change are already published for TCGA RNA-seq, including Climente-González et al. 2017 and Vitting-Seerup and Sandelin 2017. Kahles et al. 2018 quantify splicing events across 8,705 tumors and confirm a subset of neojunction peptides with CPTAC spectra. Mertins et al. 2016 connect somatic alterations to gene-level breast-cancer proteomes. Veiga et al. 2022 add long-read breast isoforms and CPTAC peptide matches. A 2026 AACR abstract describes peptide-to-isoform graphs in the ten CPTAC cohorts.

Those studies leave the frozen estimand unestablished: the partial R² of a prespecified GENCODE v34 principal-transcript proportion beyond reconstructed gene expression, tumor purity, and CNV, estimated inside each cancer and then summarized across cancers. The audit does not treat that gap as a reason to change the Phase 1A result.

Novelty grade: PARTIAL.
