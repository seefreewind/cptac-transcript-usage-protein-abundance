# Literature update

The Phase 2A audit already recorded pan-cancer isoform-switch studies, CPTAC gene-level proteogenomics, and long-read isoform resources. Two later records were checked because they sit close to this question.

Li et al., Cancer Cell, 2023, DOI 10.1016/j.ccell.2023.06.009, describe the CPTAC pan-cancer resource and the difficulty of joining transcripts to gene-level protein identifiers. Their recommendation is transparent gene-level quantification when isoform-resolved protein measurement is unrealistic. They do not estimate the partial R² of a prespecified principal-transcript proportion by structural class.

Donovan et al., PLOS ONE, 2023, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0282821, report opposite peptide-abundance patterns for BMP1 isoforms in 188 plasma proteomes. That is a single-protein, peptide-level case study. It is not a ten-cohort test of principal-transcript partial R².

The joint estimand in this phase remains unestablished by the audited literature: frozen principal-transcript usage, incremental gene-level protein explanation, predicted structural class, cancer-specific contrasts, and two literal inverses. That statement is not a claim that no related study exists. Novelty stays PARTIAL.
