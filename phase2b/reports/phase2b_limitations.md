# Limitations

The structural classes are predicted from GENCODE v34 sequences and Ensembl 100 Pfam 32.0, SignalP 4.1, and TMHMM 2.0c coordinates. Level 3 isoform-specific peptide evidence remains absent. A statistical association does not move a gene from Level 2 to Level 3.

Partial R² is right-skewed. The arithmetic mean, through the regression coefficient, stays the primary location. Trimmed means and high percentiles are descriptive.

Genes are repeated across cancers. The primary interval uses gene-level clustering. Patient-level observations were not resampled as if they were genes.

S1 is not a functionally neutral class. It contains sequence differences that the audited tracks do not mark. S0 contains measured alternatives with the same predicted amino-acid sequence.

Residue-level domain truncation was not called. Exact sequence equality does not place domain coordinates from two different sequences onto one alignment.

Candidate genes: 10795. S2 4829. S1 4779. S0 1181. Unresolved 6.
